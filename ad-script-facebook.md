# CP Loadout — Facebook feed ad script

**Format:** 45–60s, voiceover + live footage, captions burned in (most FB feed views are muted — script works with subtitles alone).

**Camera:** Fixed top-down on the table throughout. Arms/hands only — no face. One setup, no camera moves; all pacing comes from cuts and hand action.

**Concept:** One continuous build. Prototype box out, Really Useful Box in, kit assembled piece by piece on camera. Credibility comes from the prototypes — no claims needed, the evidence is on the table.

---

## HOOK — 0:00–0:04

**Visual:** Top-down. A box heaped with failed prototypes lands in shot, dropped from just off-frame.

**VO:** "This is a box of prototypes waiting to be recycled."

---

## STORY / PROBLEM — 0:04–0:15

**Visual:** Hand drags the prototype box out of shot. Beat. A Really Useful Box slides into view.

**VO:** "I designed this for myself. Dice storage. Somewhere to keep a dice tray. Token storage. All compact enough to fit the box I already carry my minis in. This box."

---

## SOLUTION / BUILD — 0:15–0:48

**Visual:** Real-time build, one component per step, cut tight. Each step gets an on-screen chip matching the site configurator ("STEP 1 — REALLY USEFUL TRAY", etc.) so the ad *is* a walkthrough of the buying flow. End on the packed box.

**VO:** "This is CP Loadout — and you build yours the same way I built mine. Five steps.

Step one — the tray. Sized to sit flush in the A4 paper tray of a Really Useful Box. *(tray drops in, flush fit shown)*

Step two — your dice tray. 12mm, 16mm, or mixed. *(dice tray in)*

Step three — your insert. The Loadout token holder, measuring gauges built in. *(holder docks in)*

Step four — tokens, pick and mix. 24 designs, Age of Sigmar or 40K, from 30p each. *(tokens slot into holder)*

Step five — extras, if you want them. *(extra placed, lid closes)*

Lid on. Army, dice, tokens — one box."

---

## CTA — 0:48–0:58

**Visual:** Hand pats the closed box. Cut to (or picture-in-picture) screen capture of the configurator stepping 1→5. URL on screen.

**VO:** "Every piece in that recycling box got it to this. Same five steps on the site — build yours at cploadout.com."

**On-screen text:** cploadout.com — Build your Loadout in 5 steps

---

## Verify before recording

- "24 designs, from 30p each" — confirm current pick & mix count/price.
- Physical build order vs configurator order: on the site the RUB tray is step 1, but physically the dice tray may need to go in before/under the insert ("lift & roll" — holder sits over the dice tray). Rehearse so the on-camera order looks natural while chips still read 1→5.
- Step 5 extras: decide which extra to show on camera (objective case or fury tracker).
- Word count ~150 → ~55s at natural VO pace. If tight, cut step five's VO line and show it with the chip only.
